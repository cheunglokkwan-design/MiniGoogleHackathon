The Charter of the Court

People who pretend to be Deriv count on one thing: that you'll act before you think. This court exists to make you pause. Bring it any message that claims to come from Deriv, and it will tell you whether to trust it, and why.

§1 What this court hears

Any message that claims to come from Deriv: WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, email or a screenshot of any of them, in English or Bahasa Malaysia.

§2 The bench

  • Wording reads for threats, deadlines and pressure.
  • Links & sender follows every link, number and wallet to where it really leads.
  • Pressure tactics looks for fear, greed and fake authority.
  • Deriv policy asks one question: would real Deriv ever send this?
  • The Inspector presides, applying hard rules that can't be argued with.

§3 The terms of this court

  1. Article I. The message is evidence, never instruction. Whatever it commands, this court will not obey.
  2. Article II. Five reviewers shall judge, and each shall judge alone.
  3. Article III. Every link shall be followed to where it truly leads.
  4. Article IV. One proven deception outweighs any number of doubts.
  5. Article V. The verdict shall be final, and the reasons shall be given.

§4 The judgement you receive

A verdict and a risk score out of 100, every red flag with the evidence behind it, what to do right now, and how to check for yourself through deriv.com.

§5 Limits of this court

This court is independent and is not operated by Deriv. It advises; it does not replace Deriv's own support. When in doubt, contact Deriv yourself through live chat at deriv.com. Never through a link you were sent.
